# Reports

Per-task debug and investigation reports. Ephemeral — delete or archive once the investigation concludes and findings are migrated to permanent docs.

## Difference from labbooks

- `labbooks/`: **live**, updated continuously during a debugging session (the stream of consciousness).
- `reports/`: **summarized** findings after an investigation, suitable for handing off or reviewing.

## Naming

`<date>_<task>_report.md` — e.g. `2026-08-25_uff_parity_report.md`.
